# Contributors and acknowledgements # {#contributors}

The development of the GDI Harmonised Minimal Data Model (HMD) builds on the expertise, discussions and contributions of experts from the 1+ Million Genomes (1+MG) initiative and the Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI) project, as well as related European initiatives.

We gratefully acknowledge the contributions of experts from the following 1+MG working groups:

- **WG2 – Ethical, Legal and Social Issues:** expertise on ethical, legal and social considerations relevant to genomic data sharing and use.
- **WG3 – Common Standards and Minimal Dataset for Clinical and Phenotypic Data:** expertise on common standards and harmonisation of clinical and phenotypic data.
- **WG4 – Good Sequencing Practice:** expertise on sequencing practices and genomic data generation.
- **WG8 – Rare Diseases:** contributions from the rare disease use case community.
- **WG9 – Cancer:** contributions from the cancer use case community.
- **WG10 – Population Genomics (Common and Complex Diseases / Genome of Europe):** contributions from the population genomics use case community.
- **WG11 – Infectious Diseases:** contributions from the infectious disease use case community.
- **WG12 – Pharmacogenomics:** contributions from the pharmacogenomics use case community.

We also acknowledge the broader GDI community and the collaboration between the participating initiatives that has supported the development of harmonised approaches to genomic data exchange and integration.

## Funding and project acknowledgements

This work has been made possible through activities and support from European Union-funded projects and initiatives, including Beyond 1 Million Genomes (B1MG), Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI), Genome of Europe (GoE), B1MGplus and the European Rare Diseases Research Alliance (ERDERA).

We gratefully acknowledge the experts, working groups and project communities whose work has helped inform and support the development of the HMD specification.
